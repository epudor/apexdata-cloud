> "What is our advertising spend actually returning?"

Advertising is one of the few line items where a company can spend heavily every month and still not be able to say, with evidence, what it bought. Platform dashboards report the numbers most favorable to the platform. Attribution windows overlap. Organic and paid revenue blur together. The result is a budget defended by anecdote.

Apex Data Cloud's advertising practice exists to settle that question. We provide **advertising measurement, analytics, and advisory services** — building the reporting and modeling layer that shows what each advertising dollar returns, so advertisers can move budget with confidence and defend the decision afterward.

## What we provide

**Retail media and Amazon Ads measurement.** Performance reporting and analysis of advertising activity across retail media networks, including Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, and Amazon DSP campaigns — broken out by campaign, ad group, product, and portfolio.

**Advertising data pipelines.** Automated ingestion of advertising reporting data via platform APIs, including the Amazon Ads API, landing in the advertiser's own warehouse. Measurement runs on infrastructure the client owns, on a schedule they control, joined to their sales and CRM data.

**Attribution and incrementality.** Multi-touch attribution and marketing mix modeling to allocate credit across the journey, plus geo tests, holdouts, and lift studies to establish what advertising genuinely added. Attribution shows correlation; incrementality proves cause.

**ROAS, ACOS and TACoS reporting.** Ad-efficiency dashboards tracking return on ad spend, advertising cost of sale, and total advertising cost of sale — so the trade-off between efficiency and growth is visible rather than argued.

**Budget and bid-strategy modeling.** Spend allocation analysis and marginal-return curves that show where the next dollar performs best, and bid-strategy recommendations that advertisers and their agencies use to plan and pace media budgets.

**Advertising audience design.** Behavioral segmentation, cohort definitions, and lookalike audience construction built specifically for advertising targeting and suppression.

## How we work

Engagements follow the same four-phase model as the rest of our practice: **Audit & Diagnose** the current advertising measurement stack and where it misleads, **Strategy & Blueprint** the measurement architecture, **Build & Deploy** the pipelines and models, and **Measure & Scale** against KPIs agreed before work begins.

We work inside your existing stack — advertising platforms, warehouses, and BI tools you already run — rather than replacing them.
